# Design review: a more creative, animated look (for Ray to decide)

Written 22 October 2026. Nothing here is switched on beyond what Batch 13 already added. Pick what you like.

## Already added in Batch 13 (small, safe)
- Browse covers rise in one after another, lift on hover and lean toward the pointer.
- Tribute pages have a drawn, gently moving hero (waves, floating candles, drifting stars).
- The welcome guide icon pops in; the hover tab bar slides down from the top edge.
- All motion stops for people who set "reduce motion" in their system.

## Ideas, from small to large
1. **Page transitions.** A short fade and slide when you open a title (already used between main pages; extend to item pages).
2. **Cover colour.** Take the main colour of a cover and tint the title page, the button and the progress bar with it.
3. **Live shelf.** Covers that gently float and show a soft shine when you move the pointer across a row.
4. **Motion backgrounds.** Slow starfield, drifting petals or rain behind Home, chosen by theme (Starry already has stars).
5. **Mood themes.** One-tap "cinema night", "rainy day", "sunrise" colour and sound sets.
6. **Streak and milestone moments.** A small celebration when you finish a series or reach a reading goal.

## Cost to watch
Animations use battery and can feel busy on older phones. Each one above can be switched off in Settings → Look if you pick it. I'd start with 2 and 1.
