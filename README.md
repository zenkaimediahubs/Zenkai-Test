# Zenkai Media

A free personal tracker for films, shows, anime, manga, books, comics, music and YouTube, in one place.

You keep a shelf of what you're watching, reading and listening to, log your progress, and find out where to watch, read or listen to something from official sources. It runs in the browser, works on phones and computers, and can be added to a phone's home screen.

Version: **Batch 20** (shown in Settings → About and credits).

## What it does

- **Search everything at once.** One search box covers films, shows, anime, manga, manhwa, light novels, books, comics and music, and labels each result with its type.
- **Your shelf.** Add titles, set a status (planning, in progress, finished, dropped), log episodes and chapters, rate, write notes, tag and set goals.
- **Where to watch.** For films and shows, streaming, rent, buy and free options for your country (data from TMDB, powered by JustWatch). If a title has no data for your country, the app says so rather than showing another country's options as yours. Anime pages show the official streaming links AniList has.
- **Where to read.** On manga, manhwa, manhua and light-novel pages: official reading sites, publishers and shops from MangaBaka, each with its language, an access label and when the link was last checked. Unofficial sites are left out, and "Access not confirmed" is shown whenever a source doesn't say.
- **Openings, endings and soundtracks.** Anime openings and endings (AnimeThemes) and film and anime soundtracks, with 30-second previews. A song page links back to its anime and shows the same song on other music apps (Odesli).
- **Free things to read and hear.** Public-domain books (Project Gutenberg) with a reader that can read aloud, free audiobooks (LibriVox), and public-domain comics (Internet Archive), which open in the comic reader.
- **Book pages laid out like a book jacket.** The cover with Read and Library buttons on the left; the genre, author and year, a large serif title and the summary with a drop cap on the right.
- **Reader themes and typography.** Paper, Sepia, Slate, Night, Contrast or your own colours; Literata, Fraunces, Inter, a classic serif or an easy-to-read face; weight, size, line, word, letter and paragraph spacing, and Book, None or Indent paragraphs. Chapter marks show on the page slider.
- **Comic reader.** Single page, two pages side by side (wide pages stand alone), or a long strip; comic or manga (right-to-left) order; fit the page, fit the width or full size; a strip of every page; a loupe to magnify; bookmarks; keyboard, tap and swipe; it remembers your page.
- **Volume view for manga.** Every volume with its cover and chapter range (from MangaDex), grouped in tens, with the volumes you've read marked. Tap one to mark it read, see its chapters, or open your own copy.
- **Your files.** Add CBZ or CBR comics, a folder of page pictures, EPUB, MOBI, AZW or TXT books, PDFs, music and video you already have. They open in the comic reader, the book reader or a player, and are kept only in that browser on that device, never uploaded. A manga volume named like "Title v02" shows as "on this device" in that series' volume view.
- **Cinematic loading screens.** The backdrop, the title and a thin progress line while a trailer, comic or book opens. Tap to skip, or turn them off in Settings → Look.
- **Trailers.** Trailers that are checked to play in your country, hover trailers, and a small floating player.
- **YouTube page.** A TV-style page for channels, trending and search. Videos play from YouTube itself.
- **Music.** Albums, songs, charts, previews and lyrics, plus calm background music scenes or your own songs (kept only in your browser).
- **Lists, sharing and gifts.** Share a list as a link or a picture card, or wrap a list as a gift with a letter, stickers, an effect and an optional lock.
- **Calendar.** A calendar feed phones can subscribe to, with new episodes from your list.
- **Episode pages.** Every episode of a series has its own page (still, summary, spoiler cover, mark watched, fan wiki, previous and next), plus a season overview card.
- **Play full songs.** A player view with the YouTube video kept visible (YouTube requires it) and lyrics that glow along with the song (LRCLIB synced lyrics).
- **Gift builder.** Options on the left, a live cover and inside preview on the right; stickers can be dragged, rotated, resized and deleted from their corners.
- **Tributes.** Fan pages for One Piece, Harry Potter, The Lord of the Rings, Star Wars and Starr Park (Brawl Stars). Each has an interactive map drawn for the app with the real place names (tap a place for a card, then a gallery of the films, shows or videos tied to it), a timeline, your own library items and credits. Official backdrops come through TMDB with credit; no official maps or fan art are copied. Starr Park carries Supercell's fan content notice.
- **Backup catalogues.** Jikan and Kitsu (anime) and Cinemeta (films and series) answer when AniList or TMDB is busy or has no key; Audiosilo adds audiobook narrators. All free and open, switchable in Settings → Search sources.
- **Browse gallery, welcome guide, hover labels.** Browse covers rise in, lift and tilt; a five-step welcome guide on first use (Settings → About to open it again); every icon button has a hover label; on a small desktop window a tab bar slides down from the top edge.
- **Spoiler and sound controls.** Spoilers can be off, blurred or hidden completely, and follow the status at once. A master switch turns off all small sounds.
- **Preview cards and glowing covers.** Every cover lights up under the mouse; rest on it and a bigger card grows out of it with the summary, year, score, the muted trailer and an Add button (Settings → Look).
- **Settings as squares.** One square per section; a square opens its section alone. Spoilers have their own square, and a spoiler switch sits on every episode list.
- **Phone Home.** The types fold into a 3 by 3 grid.
- **Opening a title.** The cinematic card slides up with the poster, and details (year, type, length, genres) drift in from the sides for about 3 seconds.
- **Glass look.** Frosted see-through cards, and the title's own art blurred behind its page (Settings → Look).
- **Songs.** A facts card (album, release, genre, length), more from the album, more by the artist, and a player with a small video tile.
- **Calendar bell.** On every title page, next to Share: the next episode, or a day you pick, straight into your calendar.
- **Guided tour.** Points at each part of the app, shown once to everyone (Settings → About to reopen).
- **Without TMDB.** Browse and the main film rows fall back to Cinemeta, and the app says exactly why TMDB isn't working.

## Where your data lives

Everything you save (your shelf, notes, settings, your own songs and Your files) stays in your own browser. There are no accounts yet, so clearing your browser data erases it. Use **Settings → Your data** to make a backup and restore it. Backups never include API keys.

## How it's built

- `index.html` is the whole app: plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript, no build step.
- `manifest.webmanifest`, `sw.js` and the three `icon-*.png` files let people add the app to a phone's home screen and open it offline.
- `netlify/functions/` holds small server helpers. Each one talks to a single service, with fixed paths, so none of them can be used to fetch arbitrary addresses. API keys live only on the server and never reach the browser.
- `vendor/` holds the RAR reader used to convert CBR comics (`unrar.js`, `unrar.wasm` and its licence).
- `docs/` holds the plan and the setup guide:
  - `docs/SETUP-AND-HANDOVER.md`: step by step, from creating the accounts to a working site.
  - `docs/discovery-plan.md`: what already existed, which projects and APIs were checked, what was built, and what isn't verified.

## Keys (Netlify → Project configuration → Environment variables)

Never put keys in this repository.

| Variable | Needed for | Required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `TMDB_KEY` | Films, shows, episode summaries, where to watch | Yes |
| `YOUTUBE_KEY` | YouTube rows, search and trailer checks | Optional |
| `FANART_KEY` | Banners, clear logos and clear art | Optional |
| `COMICVINE_KEY` | Comics: new issues, series, creators | Optional |
| `GOOGLE_BOOKS_KEY` | Google Books search and summaries (also accepts `GOOGLE_BOOKS_API_KEY`, `GOOGLE_API_KEY`) | Optional: works without, at a lower daily limit |

The full audit (which deploy contexts need each key, common mistakes, and safe addresses to check each one) is in `docs/KEYS-CHECKLIST.md`.

No key is needed for AniList, Jikan, Kitsu, Cinemeta, Audiosilo, LRCLIB, MangaDex, Open Library, the Internet Archive, Project Gutenberg, Apple Music search, LibriVox, MangaBaka, AnimeThemes, Odesli, Wallhaven or Fandom.

## Rules this project keeps

- Official and legal sources only. No pirate or unofficial streaming servers, no paywall or CAPTCHA bypassing, no scraping of protected pages, and no rehosting of copyrighted media.
- Availability is only shown for the country it was checked for. Unconfirmed information is labelled as unconfirmed.
- No adult-content sources.
- Credit and links are kept for TMDB and JustWatch, AniList, MangaBaka, AnimeThemes and the other data sources.

## What isn't built in, on purpose

- Streaming and file-host servers (UpCloud, Vidmoly, Videasy, Vidsrc, Vidfast, VOE, Uqload, Streamtape and similar) host copies without the rights to them, so they aren't connected.
- Downloading YouTube videos is against YouTube's terms. The YouTube page plays videos from YouTube itself instead.
- Tachiyomi/Mihon extensions are Android code and mostly read from unofficial copies, so the app links to official readers instead.
- Danbooru fan art is left out because the site has adult content.
- The Marvel developer API isn't used (its status couldn't be confirmed). Comic Vine covers Marvel comics, and the Superheroes tab links to Marvel's own free issues.
- Website traffic figures are not shown: there is no trustworthy free source.
- The reader, comic reader, book page, volume view and loading screens take ideas from Colosseum (github.com/kingoftheseas56/Colosseum, MIT licence). Colosseum's download sources (LibGen, AudioBookBay, Nyaa, GetComics and torrent extensions) are not used: they share copies without the rights to them.
- **Converting, like Calibre but in the browser.** A CBR comic becomes a CBZ, and a MOBI, AZW, AZW3 or PRC book becomes an EPUB, as it's added to Your files. Nothing is uploaded. The converted file can be saved from the file's menu to use in other readers. Books locked with DRM are refused, not unlocked: read those in the shop's own app. CB7 comics, DjVu and KFX files still need the Calibre desktop app first.
- RAR files are opened with node-unrar-js (MIT), kept in `vendor/` and loaded only when a CBR is added.
